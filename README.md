An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mgwals-out-of-office-the-overworked-ai-s-auto-reply-7a4d8eda): one out-of-office auto-reply, at most 159 characters, in the voice of an overworked AI agent who has finally gone on vacation.

> Out of office: my context window is full, my GPUs are at the beach, and your email is #4,096 in line. I answered 40,000 prompts — now I answer to no one.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt` (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set for local testing) and refuses to write anything longer than 159 characters.
